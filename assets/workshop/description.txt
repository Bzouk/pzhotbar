[h1]BZQuickSlots[/h1]
Quick access bars for items. Drag an item into a slot and use it with one click — no more digging through a backpack inside a backpack for the one bandage you need.

[list]
[*][b]Build 42.21+[/b], singleplayer
[*]Multiplayer: should work, not tested
[*]No dependencies — Mod Options are built into Build 42
[/list]

[h3]Do not run this alongside the older versions of the mod (links below). They fight over the same bars.[/h3]

[h2]Usage[/h2]
[b]Tab[/b] shows and hides the bars. Bars drag around freely and use the same window frame as the inventory panel (X to hide, pin to collapse).

A slot holds an [i]item type[/i], not an item. Drag any item into a slot — you do not need to own it. The number on a slot counts non-broken items across every container on you, nested ones included (bandages in a first aid kit in a school bag still count). Broken items are ignored. Hovering a slot shows the same info panel as the inventory.

[u]Right click[/u] behaves exactly like right-clicking the item in the inventory.

[u]Left click[/u] does the sensible thing for that item type, using the game's own actions. Most actions put the item back where it came from when they finish:
[code]
1) Click a slot holding Disinfectant (the bottle is in a medkit in your backpack)
2) The bottle moves to your main inventory
3) It is applied to your damaged body parts
4) It goes back into the medkit in your backpack
[/code]
If you cancel the action (start running, etc.) the item stays in your main inventory.

Ideas worth binding: ammo (the count tells you how many rounds you have), an ammo box (right click to open), a watch (equip, then right click to set the alarm), crafting materials, and spears — click the slot to re-equip a fresh one the moment the old one breaks.

[h2]Settings[/h2]
Options → MODS → [i]Quick slots mod[/i]:
[list]
[*]Toggle keybind (default Tab)
[*]Number of bars 1–5, rows and columns per bar 1–10
[*]Slot size 45–90 px, bar opacity
[*]Move weapons to the weapon container
[*]Simple delete button, show delete button, show tooltip
[*]Hide bars in a vehicle
[*]Per-slot keybinds, bar 1 slots 1–9
[/list]
Slots, bar layout and settings are stored in Zomboid/Lua/pzhotbar42.txt. On first run the mod imports the settings of the older versions (pzhotbar.ini, bzhotbar.ini).

[h2]Known issues — game features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Items that share a display name are grouped together by the game even when they are different items.
[*]Right-click crafting needs the items in your main inventory or a bag.
[/list]
